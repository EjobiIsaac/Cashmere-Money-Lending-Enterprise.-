# 💰 CASHMERE - Money Lending

**Professional Money Lending Management System**

Manage loans, clients, payments, and reports - all in one beautiful app.

![CASHMERE Icon](icon.png)

## ✨ Features
- 👥 Client Management - Add, edit & track borrowers
- 💸 Loan Management - Create loans, interest & terms
- 💳 Payment Tracking - Record payments & balances
- 📊 Dashboard - Total lent, collected & outstanding
- 📄 Reports - Daily, weekly & monthly summaries
- 💾 Auto-Save - Your data saves in browser
- 📱 Works on Phone & PC

## 🚀 How To Use

### Option 1: Use Online (Easiest)
Open this link:
**https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/cashmere-money-lending/**

### Option 2: Download Windows EXE
1. Go to **Actions** tab
2. Download **CASHMERE-EXE** artifact
3. Unzip & double-click `CASHMERE-Money-Lending.exe`

### Option 3: Run Locally
1. Download `index.html`
2. Double-click to open in browser
